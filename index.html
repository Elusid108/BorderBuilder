LithoLab pack

Import an STL zip or .litholab file. The mask becomes the opening; export sizes set the artwork and rabbet.

Shape

Artwork / sight size

Visible opening. The frame is sized from this plus moulding width.

Moulding

Lip is the flat landing on the glass (separate from the back rabbet). Face depth is how strongly the moulding reads — 0 is almost flat, 1 is full character.

Rabbet

Notch on the back-inner corner. Width is how far the pocket stands out from the opening; depth is how deep. On LithoLab imports the pocket follows the lithophane (mask + border), not a simplified outer shape.

Rectangles: shrinks the glass-size readout. LithoLab import: extra room around the lithophane + border in the rabbet (at least 0.4 mm), following that outline.
